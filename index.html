What the AI actually does

CivicEye is a decision-support system. It surfaces evidence and shows its reasoning — it doesn't claim to solve civic problems on its own, and every pattern it finds is labeled as a statistical association, not a proven cause.

01

Pattern detection

Models scan public datasets for combinations of indicators — like transport access and dropout rate — that deviate from the norm for a given area.

02

Explained, not black-boxed

Every detected pattern ships with the variables behind it and a plain-language explanation, so a non-technical reader can follow the reasoning.

03

Scenario estimates, clearly labeled

The What-If Lab shows model-based "what could change" estimates — always marked as estimates, never presented as guarantees.
